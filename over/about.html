Welcome

Government-licensed Nepal recruitment agency.

Authorized by the Department of Foreign Employment (DoFE), we provide ethical manpower supply from Nepal - delivering verified, trade-tested Nepali workers to international employers.

From demand letter to airport departure, every placement is handled with full legal compliance, honest communication and a promise that each worker travels with a genuine contract and a real job awaiting them.

The complete manpower recruitment lifecycle.
