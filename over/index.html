Who We Are

Manpower recruitment, done with integrity.

Recruitment Flow

Five stages from demand to departure.

Every placement follows the same disciplined path. The full procedure - trade tests, medicals, embassy submission, visa stamping - runs to twenty steps.

  1. 01

    Source & Register

    Demand letters are matched with registered candidates from across Nepal.

  2. 02

    Screen & Interview

    Shortlists, trade tests and interviews conducted directly with the employer.

  3. 03

    Document & Medical

    Contracts verified and government-approved medicals completed.

  4. 04

    Visa & Orientation

    Embassy submission, visa stamping and pre-departure orientation.

  5. 05

    Deploy & Support

    Flight booking, airport assistance and post-arrival follow-up.
