Who We Are

Manpower recruitment, done with integrity.

Shuvam Overseas works as a bridge between Nepali workers seeking dignified employment abroad and employers who need reliable, vetted talent. From demand letter to airport departure, every candidate is handled with full legal compliance and genuine care.

Experience

Years of hands-on practice across Gulf and Southeast Asian markets give us the judgement to recruit the right person for the right role, the first time.

Trust

Government-licensed and contract-focussed, we hold ourselves to clear agreements, fair terms and complete transparency with candidates and employers alike.

Reach

A wide candidate base across Nepal's skilled trades, hospitality, manufacturing and service sectors - matched against employers in the Gulf, Asia and beyond.
