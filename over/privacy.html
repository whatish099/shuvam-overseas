Privacy Policy

Your details, handled with care.

This page explains what information Shuvam Overseas Employment PVT.LTD collects through this website, how it is used, and the choices you have. We keep it short and plain on purpose.

1. Information we collect

When you send a message through our enquiry forms, we receive the name, email address and message content you type, plus any phone number or role you choose to share. We do not ask for payment details, passport numbers or other sensitive documents through this website.

2. How we use it

Your details are used only to reply to your enquiry - matching employers with manpower demand, registering candidate interest, or answering general questions. We do not sell, rent or trade your information to third parties.

3. Storage & preferences

This website stores one item in your browser's local storage: your language preference (English or Nepali), so the site can reopen in the language you chose. You can clear it at any time through your browser settings. Form submissions are handled on your device until a form service is connected.

4. Third-party services

Pages load typefaces from Google Fonts. External links - such as WhatsApp chat - open third-party sites with their own privacy policies. Embedded maps, where present, are served by Google and are subject to Google's terms. We add no advertising or tracking scripts of any kind.

5. Your rights

You may ask us to correct or delete any personal details we hold about you from an enquiry. Write to info@shuvamoverseas.com or call +977-95-1012541 and we will respond within one working day.

6. Contact
