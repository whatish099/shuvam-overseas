Required Documents

What a candidate needs to begin.

01Mandatory Criteria

  • Age between 18 and 45 years (varies by destination country)
  • A valid passport with at least two years of remaining validity
  • Fitness confirmed by a government-approved medical examination
  • Relevant educational certificates and/or experience letters
  • Police clearance / character certificate where required
  • Passport-size photographs as per embassy specifications

02Mandatory Legal Documents

  1. Demand Letter
  2. Employment Contract
  3. Power of Attorney
  4. Agreement Paper
  5. Guarantee Letter
  6. Letter to Embassy
  7. Letter to Ministry of Labor

Saudi Arabia

Deployment requires an E-Wakala from the Saudi employer plus a MOFA number. Our team manages the entire Saudi documentation chain in-house - see the Saudi visa process below.

Malaysia

Malaysian recruitment follows FWCMS (Foreign Worker Central Management System) procedures. The employer's FWCMS registration must be active before we begin the file.
