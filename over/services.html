Our Services

Overseas Recruitment

End-to-end manpower solutions - sourcing the right candidate, managing every legal step, and supporting the worker from training to touchdown at the destination.

Bridging the Gap

From local talent pools to global workforces.

The distance between a qualified Nepali worker and a demanding employer is more than geography - it is paperwork, verification and trust. We close that gap.

We source skilled, semi-skilled and unskilled manpower from districts across Nepal, verify every credential, and deliver a shortlist that meets the employer's exact specification - so the only thing that has to travel is the candidate themselves.

Sectors & Categories

Manpower across nine key sectors.

From specialist electricians to general labourers, our candidate base spans the categories employers ask for most.

Training & Orientation

Workers prepared for the job - and the journey.

Pre-Departure Orientation

  • Contract terms, wages and employment rights
  • Destination country culture and workplace norms
  • Safety, health and emergency procedures
  • Do's and don'ts for a successful stay

Skills Training

Where a role requires it, candidates receive practical skills training and assessment - so they arrive job-ready, not just passport-ready.

Travel Management

Every detail of the journey, handled.

  • Flight Booking Confirmed international flights at the best available fare.
  • Visa Assistance Complete preparation and submission of visa applications.
  • Pre-Departure Briefing A final walkthrough of documents, dos and don'ts before travel.
  • Airport Assistance On-ground support through check-in, immigration and boarding.
  • Transit Support Coordination with local representatives at transit and arrival.
